# Design Review: Patreon-Inspired Boundary

This packet prepares the human-in-the-loop review for Story 011. It does not replace the reviewer decision; it gives the reviewer explicit criteria for deciding whether Patreonic is premium and creator-economy oriented while remaining original.

## Human reviewer checklist

- [ ] Confirm the template feels premium and creator-economy oriented.
- [ ] Confirm the hero/header/background interactions meet the intended hero/header/background interactions category: lively, polished, scroll-aware, and creator-platform appropriate.
- [ ] Confirm the copy, visual assets, color system, typography, and layout details are distinguishable from Patreon.
- [ ] Record any required design-boundary adjustments as follow-up tasks below.

## Inspiration boundary

Patreonic may be inspired by the broad product category and interaction mood of a premium creator-economy landing page: bold editorial hero messaging, an adaptive sticky header, generated creator/media cards, pointer-responsive hero depth, and scroll-linked atmospheric background changes.

The intended result is Patreon-inspired mood, original execution.

## Originality guardrails

- Use no copied Patreon content.
- Use no Patreon logos.
- Use no screenshots or proprietary imagery.
- Use no exact colors from Patreon as a copied palette.
- Use no exact typography pairing from Patreon.
- Use no pixel-level layout reproduction.
- Keep copy, generated visuals, card shapes, section composition, color tokens, and motion implementation specific to Patreonic.

## Follow-up adjustments

- [ ] If the reviewer finds the design too close to Patreon, document the specific section, asset, copy, motion, or layout concern here before changing implementation.
- [ ] If the reviewer finds the design not premium enough, document the desired mood adjustment and whether it affects hero, header, atmosphere, generated media, copy, or typography.
- [ ] If the reviewer approves the boundary, record the reviewer name/date and mark Story 011 acceptance criteria complete in `docs/TASKS.md`.
